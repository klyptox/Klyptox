3. Client FAQ (humanized)

How are you different from an Upwork freelancer?

An Upwork freelancer is one person. We're five editors running multiple accounts. More clips out, more chances something lands, and you're not stuck waiting on a single person's schedule.

What do you charge?

$1.50 per 1,000 verified Tier-1 views, billed weekly. No retainer, no setup fee. Most clients start between $100 and $500 a week, and you only pay for views that actually land. PH clients: flat around ₱5K/week.

How do I know the views are real?

We only count verified organic views. We watch engagement, not just the number. If a spike looks padded, we filter it out before you see the bill.

Do you post on my accounts or yours?

Either. We can run yours, ours, or both. You get a weekly report either way.

What if it doesn't work?

You're not locked in. Pause anytime. Clipping usually compounds around week 3 or 4 as the accounts warm up. If it's genuinely not moving after a month, I'll tell you straight.

Do I need to give you editing guidelines?

A 10-minute call gets us about 80% there. We'll review the first batch with you before anything goes live, so you stay in control.

How fast can you start?

Send the long-form today, clips are ready in 3 to 5 days. You approve, we post.

Why should I trust a new agency?

Fair question. We lead with proof. Send us one video, we'll clip three moments free. You judge the quality yourself.

Do you only do TikTok?

No. TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Facebook. Most clients start with one or two and expand once they see numbers.

What do I need to provide?

Just the long-form. Podcasts, YouTube livestreams, interviews. Anything with a recording, we can work with it.
